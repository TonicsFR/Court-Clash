HOW TO PLAY · SKILL GUIDE

🏀 OFFENSE

AD Drive. Double-tap a direction to SPRINT (burns stamina).

Hold W → jump shot. Release the needle in the GREEN to score. The bright center strip is PERFECT: a clean swish that builds your ZONE (bigger green, stacks 3×).

APEX: releasing at the very top of your jump makes the green 25% bigger. The meter glows when you're there.

Tap W → PUMP FAKE. Make defenders jump, then walk past them or shoot over them.

S while moving → CROSSOVER. Cross against the defender's momentum to break their ANKLES.

S again right after a crossover → SPIN MOVE.

S standing still → STEPBACK: the next shot gets a bigger green.

SET FEET: standing still before you shoot gives a bonus. Shooting on the move, contested or tired shrinks the green.

W near the rim → DUNK. Let go of W when the shrinking ring hits the ball for an unblockable POWER DUNK.

🛡 DEFENSE

Stay in front. A close defender shrinks the shooter's green. Jumping makes the contest even stronger.

W Block. Time your jump with their release. Jump too early, or bite on a pump fake, and they get an easy look.

S Steal. Works best when the ball is low on the bounce and on your side. Swiping during their crossover gets your ankles broken.

Don't over-commit: missed steals slow you down, and every move costs stamina.

⚡ STAMINA

Your bar sits under your feet. Sprinting, crossovers, spins, steals and jumps all use it. Below 20% you're slower and your shots get worse.

🔥 STREAKS

3 buckets in a row = ON FIRE (bigger green). 3 from beyond the arc. Tied at the buzzer = sudden death.
